## 0.1.0 - 2026-08-01

### Added

- A dedicated Electronics Printer kit and placeable bridge cloned from the vanilla Logic Mirror.
- All, prefab-hash, and case-insensitive packed-string name filters.
- Transparent direct, batch, reference-ID, write, and slot access to imported devices.
- Server-authoritative Mode and Setting configuration with save/reload persistence.
- Support for output-local devices, chained bridges, duplicate paths, and cyclic topologies.
- Listen-server and dedicated-server compatibility.
